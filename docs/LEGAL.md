# PricePilot AI - Legal, Compliance & Affiliate Disclosures

**Last Updated:** October 2026

PricePilot AI is committed to transparency, user privacy, ethical data sourcing, and strict compliance with Indian laws, advertising guidelines (ASCI), and partner platform terms of service.

---

## 1. Affiliate Disclosure & Commercial Transparency

In compliance with the **Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI)** Guidelines for Influencer and Digital Advertising and the **US Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** 16 CFR § 255:

* **Commercial Relationship:** PricePilot AI participates in various affiliate marketing programs, including but not limited to the **Cuelinks Affiliate Network**, **Amazon Associates India**, and direct retailer affiliate programs.
* **No Additional Cost:** When users click on outbound links leading to partner merchant stores (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales) and make a qualifying purchase, PricePilot AI may earn an affiliate commission. This incurs **no additional cost** to the consumer.
* **Editorial Independence:** PricePilot's recommendation algorithms—specifically the **Smart Buy Score**, **Deal Honesty Engine**, **Wait Forecaster**, and **Personal Buy Decision** verdicts—are 100% deterministic and mathematically driven. Retailers cannot pay for higher rankings, inflated scores, or preferential placement.

## 2. Data Sourcing & Scraping Compliance

* **API-First Architecture:** PricePilot AI sources real-time product discovery, catalog listings, and pricing information primarily through authorized APIs and search indexing partners (SerpApi, Serper, SearchApi, and DataForSEO).
* **Robots.txt & Fair Use:** Where direct discovery takes place, PricePilot AI adheres strictly to public `robots.txt` directives, implements strict client rate-limiting, and utilizes aggressive caching layers (TTL 6–24 hours) to eliminate redundant queries and prevent server load on merchant platforms.
* **No Captcha Circumvention:** PricePilot does not bypass authentication barriers, CAPTCHAs, or anti-bot protections. If merchant data is inaccessible via public search indexers, it gracefully falls back to cached records or notifies the user of limited availability.

## 3. Data Accuracy Disclaimer

* **Dynamic Pricing:** Prices, coupon validity, bank card discount terms, cashback rates, and product stock levels on e-commerce platforms change dynamically.
* **Informational Purpose Only:** All price comparisons, drop probabilities, festival forecasts, and net effective price calculations generated by PricePilot AI are provided for **informational and decision-support purposes only**.
* **Merchant Final Authority:** The merchant's checkout page remains the sole binding offer. PricePilot AI cannot guarantee that a listed price or coupon code will be honored by a third-party merchant at checkout.

## 6. Financial Data Privacy & Security

PricePilot's **Payment Method & Card Benefit Optimization** feature helps users calculate bank discounts (e.g., "5% cashback on Amazon Pay ICICI", "10% instant discount on HDFC Millennia"):

* **Zero Card Number Storage:** PricePilot **NEVER** collects, requests, stores, or processes 16-digit primary account numbers (PAN), expiration dates, PINs, or CVV/CVC codes.
* **Metadata Only:** The system only records the **Bank Issuer** (e.g., *HDFC Bank*), **Card Name/Tier** (e.g., *Millennia Credit Card*), and **Network** (e.g., *Visa* / *RuPay*).
* **Document Parsing Security:** When users utilize the Gemini Vision statement or invoice scanner:
  * Scanned documents are parsed in-memory using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.
  * Extracted purchase date, product name, and net price are saved to the user's Price Protection records.
  * Raw image/PDF files are never persistently stored on public servers or shared with third-party advertisers.
